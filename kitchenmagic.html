Yummy Smoothie

Non-Dairy Smoothie

Ingredients

  • fruit of your choice(apple,mango,pineapple, strawberries, banana and blueberries)
  • 1/4 cup fruit juice (apple,pear,or white grape)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Fruit for garnish

Instructions

Peel, seed, and dice the fruit. Place all ingredients into a blender. Cover and blend. Pour into glass and garnish with fruit. Sit back and enjoy the yummy smoothie.
