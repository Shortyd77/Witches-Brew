Honey Touch

A sweetening spell to reconcile or fix problems in a relationship with honey and the person name.

Supplies
One red candle
Honey
Boline or kitchen knife

Instructions

  1. Get a red candle and carve your full name on one side, and your loved one's name on the other side. For this, you can use your boline, knife, or toothpick.
  2. Put some honey on your fingertips and gently dress the candle for a sweetening effect. While you do this think of the sweetest thoughts and images, projecting them onto that person.
  3. Place the candle on your altar on a dish/candle holder.
  4. Light the candle. As it burns,breathe slowly and visualize the beautiful moments in your relationship. Focus on these feelings and what you truly want for your relationship.
  5. Meditate for 10 minutes and then snuff out the candle
  6. Light it back the following day and repeat the chant. Do this everyday until candle has extinguished completely. Over time you will see your connection with your partner improve.

As you light the candle chant: "A spell for the new, as bitterness departs from you. A spell for the old, as honey touches your soul."

Source: Spells.com

Divination Oil

This oil is great for meditation, encouraging mental awareness, prophetic dreams, and psychic abilities.

Use any combination of the following herbs:

  • Bay leaves
  • Cinnamon
  • Cloves
  • Hibiscus
  • Jasmine
  • Lemon Balm
  • Oregano

Steps

  • You will need glass bottle, Olive oil, Vitamin E oil, and herbs of choice
  • Add the dry ingredients to a clean glass jar or bottle, thanking each ingredient for its helpful qualities.
  • Top it up with your base oil. Other options are grape-seed oil or almond oil.
  • Add a few drops of vitamin E oil to preserve it.
  • Seal the bottle and give it a good shake.
  • Light a purple candle
  • Place any crystals you find helpful next to it. Such as Amethyst, Labradorite, or Moonstone.
  • Meditate with your new oil, asking for clarity, increased psychic abilities, expanded awareness and inner wisdom.
  • Give it a good shake every few days for the next month

How to use the oil, use for your Third Eye.Hold the bottle and look into the flame of the candle anoint it with oil while you chant:

"Open my eyes, Open my mind. The truth that I seek, Help me to find."

Fresh Funds

Herbal Spell to Attract Money

Place a bill of high denomination inside a chest or box. On top of the bill, place 1 whole garlic.

Add 5 basil leaves on top of the bill. Place a white handkerchief on top and pour 3 or 4 drops of prosperity oil (such as patchouli, bergamot, cinnamon, sandalwood, myrrh, or ginger). Close the chest and keep it under your bed. Let it sit at least one full moon cycle (28 days) and don't touch it

Chant: "Tis time, tis time to connect and to combine. Timeless Magic, fresh allure, funds increase and stay secure"
